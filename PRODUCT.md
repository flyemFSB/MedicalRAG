# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Clinicians and medical researchers ask questions at a desk or in a clinic, mid-session, and need a calm, trustworthy surface for exploring curated medical knowledge. Knowledge maintainers and platform operators use the same workbench to ingest documents, inspect chunks, edit the intent tree, and review model health. Every screen serves the task: read evidence, verify the answer, or keep the knowledge base healthy.

## Product Purpose

MedicalRAG is an evidence-first medical knowledge workbench: a chat interface plus an operator console that helps clinicians, researchers, and knowledge maintainers explore curated medical knowledge without ever presenting unsupported output as diagnosis or prescription. It reimplements the Ragent RAG platform with behavioral parity, adds a medical safety boundary, and keeps data on the operator's own infrastructure. Success is a clinician who can ask a question, inspect the evidence behind the answer, and trust both the answer and its limits.

## Positioning

Every answer is traceable to evidence and stays within a safety boundary — MedicalRAG is a reliable knowledge assistant, never an autonomous clinician. The interface reinforces one claim: **the evidence is inspectable, the limits are explicit, and the tool will not overstep.**

## Brand Personality

Credible, restrained, clear. The voice is that of a dependable clinical colleague: direct, specific, and quietly confident — no hype, no whimsy, no opacity. Calm precision from a monochrome, continuous-canvas surface; authority from Geist typography and a single Vercel-blue accent.

## Anti-references

- Not an outdated hospital-admin console: no stale widgets, muddy colors, or heavy tables.
- Not a generic AI chatbot: no decorative gradients, emoji, cartoon avatars, or glassmorphism.
- Not a dark developer-tool surface: no neon, high-saturation, or terminal-native styling.

## Design Principles

- **Evidence is the interface.** Sources, provenance, and citation labels are first-class and always reachable from an answer; retrieval and safety states are explicit, not hidden.
- **Restraint is authority.** A monochrome palette with one functional accent (Vercel blue), hairline borders on a continuous canvas, and Geist typography. The tool disappears into the task.
- **Clinical-grade clarity.** High contrast, dense-but-readable operator tables, fixed type scale, and a safety boundary that is the single most visible element when it appears.
- **Statefulness over decoration.** Motion and color convey status only (150–250ms, skeleton loading, semantic states); nothing animates for its own sake.
- **Consistency across surfaces.** The same button, form, and status vocabulary in chat and in the operator console — familiarity is trust.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Body and secondary text meet 4.5:1; a visible focus ring on every interactive element; full keyboard navigation; status never conveyed by color alone; and a reduced-motion variant for all animation.
