# The product uses a fixed medical AI disclaimer

MedicalRAG displays the fixed Chinese disclaimer **AI生成内容仅供参考，不可替代医嘱，请以医生诊断为准** as persistent product copy in the medical chat experience and associated answer context. The disclaimer is rendered by the UI, is accessible to screen readers, and is not inserted into the model's answer text or treated as a substitute for the Safety Boundary. It does not authorize diagnosis, prescribing, dosage calculation, emergency exclusion, or other Clinical Decision behavior that the product explicitly forbids.

## Consequences

- The wording is versioned product copy and must not vary by model response or be generated by the LLM.
- The disclaimer remains visible during loading, streaming, error, empty-evidence, and completed states.
- Tests verify its presence, exact text, accessible semantics, and behavior across responsive layouts.
- Additional safety guidance may appear for a high-risk Intent, but it cannot remove or weaken this baseline notice.
